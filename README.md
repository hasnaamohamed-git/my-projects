## 📁 Projects

### 1. **Weather Analysis** (`weather analysis.zip`)
**Climate Impact on Egyptian Agriculture and Business** 
 analyzing how weather and climate variables changed in Egypt from 2015 to 2024 and their effects on agriculture, business risks, and urban planning. Alexandria is used as a detailed coastal case study.

- **Key Analyses**: Soil moisture variance across governorates, temperature trends , precipitation patterns, humidity, and multi-variable insights.
- **Tools**: python 
- **Main Takeaway**: Climate change is creating both risks (e.g., flooding in coastal areas) and new opportunities (e.g., improved soil moisture in Alexandria).
